# Pandemic Simulation System

A system designed to simulate the spread of a pandemic across various regions and analyze the effects of different intervention strategies, such as lockdowns, vaccinations, and health care resource allocation. The system continuously adapts its predictions based on incoming data, providing suggestions for optimal policies to minimize the pandemic's impact.

## Features

1. **Infection Spread Simulation**  
   Simulates the transmission of the virus through different regions, accounting for population density, travel routes, and other factors.

2. **Dynamic Interventions**  
   Allows users to implement and adjust strategies like lockdowns, vaccination campaigns, and resource distribution in real time.

3. **Infection Trends and Statistics**  
   Visualizes the growth of infection rates, recoveries, and mortality across time, providing detailed statistical insights.

4. **Customizable Infection Rates**  
   Adjust infection rates, mortality rates, and recovery times to model different pandemic scenarios and variants.

5. **Recovery Simulation**  
   Models the recovery process, tracking individuals through infection stages and accounting for healthcare availability.

6. **Add and Remove Regions**  
   Dynamically expand or reduce the simulation's scope by adding or removing regions as needed.

7. **Optimized Route and Policy Suggestions**  
   Provides suggestions for travel restrictions, resource allocation, and health policies based on real-time data.

8. **Save and Load Simulation State**  
   Save the current state of the simulation and reload it later to continue analysis or refine strategies.

9. **Adjustments Based on New Data**  
   Automatically adjusts predictions and policies as new data is introduced, ensuring the simulation stays up-to-date with the latest information.
